Reference recordings for announcement filing.

Put a WAV (8 kHz mono 16-bit, as saved in recordings/) of a network
announcement here, named after it - e.g. subscriber-unreachable.wav.
Saved voice calls that match one (by spectral shape of the speech) are
filed under recordings/announcements/<name>/ instead of recordings/ and
can be hidden in the Voice tab. With no files here the feature is inert.
